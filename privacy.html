Privacy Policy

When you join the lab notes list we collect the email address you enter, plus anything you choose to add in the optional fields: sport preference and whatever you write in the box.

That form is submitted to Buttondown, the service that sends the lab notes, and your details are stored there on our behalf. We use your email only to send lab notes when something changes, and to tell you first if a rule ever clears the bar. We do not sell it. We do not share it for advertising.

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you are off the list. You can also email support.pixelforgeplays@gmail.com and we will remove you.

Two other forms are run on Tally: the form to race your model and the form to dispute a grade. They collect what you type in, including your email if you give it. We use it only to answer you.

We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors.
