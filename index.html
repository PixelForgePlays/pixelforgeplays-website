The Standard is the bar a rule has to clear before we would sell its picks. Read it.

We don't grade ourselves. The close does.

The close is also called the closing line. Getting a better price than the close is the sign we look for. It shows up before wins and losses do.

How it works

  1. 1. Lock the rule. Each rule is frozen before its first game.
  2. 2. Post every pick. Before the game, losses included.
  3. 3. Let the close decide. We only sell a rule's picks if it beats the close by the margin we published in advance. Nothing has reached that margin yet.
